A bit about my experience

I've spent a good part of my career working on complicated websites and applications, including government services and enterprise software.

One thing I've learned is that something can work perfectly well from a technical standpoint and still be frustrating for the people using it. That's the kind of problem I enjoy figuring out.

A little about me

I'm Derek, a designer, developer, and UX practitioner based in Regina, Saskatchewan.

I've worked on everything from websites and interactive learning materials to complex business applications. Along the way, I've worn a lot of different hats — designing interfaces, writing code, talking to users, and testing whether things actually work for them.

I like being involved in both sides of the process: figuring out what needs to be improved and getting my hands dirty making those improvements.
